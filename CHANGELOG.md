# Changelog

## 2.0.0

- Multiple currencies with live rates, including the BCV, parallel and Binance bolívar rates, plus a converter.
- English and Spanish, with a welcome screen.
- Custom income categories and donation sites with links, colors and logos.
- Custom accent color, app icon color and left or right-hand controls.
- Show or hide amounts from any screen.
- Paste amounts from the clipboard.
- Pay schedules: weekly, every two weeks, twice a month and monthly.
- Payday notifications through a GitHub Actions job.
- Edge-to-edge layout and spring-based glass motion.
- Settings sync, backup files and unit tests.

## 1.0.0

- First version: ledger, history, giving screen, yearly summary and GitHub backup.
